## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide 16:9 background artwork for the main menu of BulletPunk, a tactical top-down cyberpunk roguelite. Primary request: an atmospheric underground operative safehouse and armory. No people, no lettering, no logos, no interface. Hand-painted cinematic game splash art with crisp architectural silhouettes, restrained teal jade and warm amber lighting, graphite metal and aged concrete. Workbench with compact futuristic firearms and equipment on the right foreground, warm hanging task lamp above it, dark glass tactical monitors and cabling on the back wall, trophy shelf and shuttered industrial window with distant rain. Wide horizontal composition with the left third quiet and dark enough for UI text, more environmental storytelling on the right. Sophisticated natural material textures, layered depth, subtle illuminated dust, inviting but dangerous world, polished illustration coherent with a cyberpunk operative portrait. Keep overall scene readable with deep shadow areas, avoid excessive neon, photorealism, floating text and characters.

La escena anima transformaciones y luz con CSS. Respeta la preferencia de movimiento reducido; el perfil Fluido detiene el desplazamiento del fondo.
